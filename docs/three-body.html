The Three-Body Problem

Two bodies orbiting under gravity trace a clean, predictable ellipse forever. Add a third and, in general, there's no equation for where they end up — this is the problem that led Poincaré to discover chaos in the first place, in 1887. Click anywhere to nudge every body's velocity a little, drag the mass sliders to reshape the gravity live, and adjust speed to slow it down around a close pass.
